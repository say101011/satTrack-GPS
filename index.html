Privacy Policy

How location, camera, sensor, online map and advertising information is handled in SatTrack GPS.

Last updated: September 23, 2026 · App version 1.2.2
Package: com.aistudio.satfinder.krvwpx

SatTrack GPS is not a fully offline app.
GNSS reception, satellite and sensor displays, and AR processing can work without internet access. New map images and advertisements require an internet connection. Your device’s network-based location services may also communicate over the network.

Location & camera

The app processes current location in memory. It does not create location-history logs or record or upload camera footage.

Online maps & advertising

Map requests disclose your IP address and the displayed area to OpenStreetMap. Google’s advertising SDK also handles information under its own policies.

1. Scope

This policy describes data handling in the SatTrack GPS Android application. The app does not provide account registration or user accounts. Information processed by map and advertising providers is also subject to their respective privacy policies.

2. Location and device permissions

Precise or approximate location

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION are used to display current location, estimated accuracy, altitude, speed and satellite status. In the Map menu, the app displays your current position and can follow location updates.
  • Approximate location permission is supported, but positioning precision and some GNSS satellite information may be limited. The map hides the current-position marker when a recent valid location is unavailable.
  • The app subscribes to location updates while it is active in the foreground. It stops its location subscriptions when it goes into the background. It does not store a travel history or coordinate logs in files or on its own server.

Camera and orientation sensors

Camera permission enables the live preview used by AR Sky View. The app does not capture, save or transmit photos or videos. Orientation sensor readings are used to align radar, 3D and AR displays and are not saved as a separate history.

Internet and network state

INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE support online maps, advertising and related network functionality. Android does not show a separate runtime permission prompt for internet access. Data charges may apply according to your service plan.

3. OpenStreetMap online maps

  • When requests occur: Opening Map or moving or zooming the map requests the images (tiles) needed for the visible area from https://tile.openstreetmap.org. Leaving the Map menu removes that map view.
  • Information disclosed: The provider receives your IP address, app/browser identification (User-Agent), request time and map tile coordinates (zoom level and grid coordinates). The displayed area may reveal or allow your location to be inferred. The app does not send raw GPS latitude and longitude as separate fields to the tile server.
  • Local cache: WebView may retain map images on your device according to the server’s HTTP caching instructions. Cached imagery can reflect areas you have viewed. You can remove it by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings. The app does not offer bulk map downloads and does not guarantee offline map availability.
  • Provider processing: The OpenStreetMap Foundation (OSMF) and its infrastructure process map requests to deliver maps. Its privacy policy also describes using request records for technical operations, security, service planning and aggregate research. Depending on your location and the network infrastructure, processing may take place outside your country. Provider logging and retention are governed by the provider’s policy; requests are not guaranteed to be processed only temporarily in memory.
  • Availability: This community map service has capacity and usage-policy limits. Access may be interrupted or withdrawn. Map accuracy and continuous service are not guaranteed.

4. Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

The app includes a Google Mobile Ads banner. The source for this version uses Google test ad identifiers. Test ads can still involve SDK network communication.

Google’s SDK may collect or share IP addresses (which may be used to estimate general location), device and advertising identifiers, app or ad interactions, and diagnostic information for purposes such as advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. This processing is separate from the app’s in-memory GNSS processing. The app does not supply precise GPS coordinates to the SDK as a separate advertising field.

5. Retention, deletion and your choices

  • Location, sensors and camera: The app processes these readings in memory for live display. It does not create a server database of these readings or a saved travel history.
  • Cached and provider data: Map caching is managed by WebView and server caching instructions. Provider logs and advertising information may be retained under each provider’s policies.
  • Change permissions: In Android, open Settings → Apps → SatTrack GPS → Permissions to change or withdraw location or camera access. Related features will be limited when access is withdrawn.
  • Delete local data: Clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the app to remove its local data. This does not delete records already held by external providers.
  • Control map traffic: The app does not request new map tiles when the Map menu is not in use. Advertisements can still communicate from other screens. Disabling the device’s network connection prevents new maps and ads from loading.
