SatTrack GPS - GNSS Satellite Tracker & AR Sky Viewer
Effective Date: March 30, 2025

GPS Location On-Device Only

Used strictly in real-time to compute GNSS satellite geometry and plot radar positions. Never saved, logged, or uploaded to any remote server.

Camera Access Zero Storage

Used solely to render real-time satellite orbital layers in AR mode over your camera view. No photos or videos are ever captured or transmitted.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how SatTrack GPS ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our") handles user information. We are committed to protecting user privacy and ensuring transparent data processing practices in full compliance with Google Play Developer Policies, GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation), CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act), and global privacy regulations.

2. Data Collection and Usage

SatTrack GPS is engineered with a privacy-first, on-device architecture. Below are details regarding device permissions utilized by the Application:

A. Precise Location Data (GNSS / GPS)

  • Permissions: ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION
  • Purpose: Access raw GNSS satellite status (GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, BeiDou, QZSS), measure carrier-to-noise ratio (C/N0), calculate positioning accuracy, and render positions on 2D Radar, 3D Globe, and AR Sky View.
  • Data Handling: Processed 100% on-device in real time while the app is in the foreground. Location coordinates are never recorded to disk, tracked as travel history, or transmitted to remote servers.

B. Camera Access (Augmented Reality Sky View)

  • Permission: CAMERA
  • Purpose: Displays live camera preview feed for AR Sky View mode to align satellite vectors against the physical sky.
  • Data Handling: Optical feeds are rendered directly on screen memory. The Application does not record videos, take static photos, or store/transmit camera streams.

C. Motion and Orientation Sensors

  • Sensors Used: Rotation Vector, Gyroscope, Accelerometer, Magnetometer
  • Purpose: Determines azimuth heading and pitch angle for AR reticle and 3D compass orientation.
  • Data Handling: Evaluated locally per frame and discarded immediately after rendering.

D. Advertising & Analytics (Google AdMob & UMP)

  • Service Provider: Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK & Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)
  • Purpose: Displays policy-compliant banner advertisements.
  • Data Collected: Google AdMob may collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic information and unique device advertising identifiers (such as Advertising ID / AD_ID) in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. Google UMP automatically manages GDPR/ePrivacy privacy choices in required regions (EEA/UK).

E. OpenStreetMap Map View

  • Permissions: INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE
  • Purpose: Renders interactive map views. Online maps are disabled by default and can be toggled in Privacy settings. Map requests disclose your IP address to OpenStreetMap tile servers to fetch map tiles.

3. Data Retention and Security

Because SatTrack GPS does not transmit personal data or location coordinates to remote servers, we do not maintain central databases of user personal information. Diagnostic calculations cease immediately upon exiting the Application.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

SatTrack GPS is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The Application requires no user account registration.

5. Your Choices & Permission Controls

You can grant, modify, or revoke permissions at any time via Android settings (Settings > Apps > SatTrack GPS > Permissions).
