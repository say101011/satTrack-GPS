Privacy Policy

SatTrack GPS - GNSS Satellite Tracker & AR Sky Viewer
Effective Date: March 30, 2025

GPS Location On-Device Only

Used strictly in real-time to compute GNSS satellite geometry and plot radar positions. Never saved, logged, or uploaded to any remote server.

Camera Access Zero Storage

Used solely to render real-time satellite orbital layers in AR mode over your camera view. No photos or videos are ever captured or transmitted.

Sensors & Heading Local Processing

Compass and gyroscope data align the 3D reticle with physical azimuth. Telemetry is discarded immediately after rendering each frame.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how SatTrack GPS ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our") handles data when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting user privacy and ensuring transparent data processing practices in full compliance with Google Play Developer Policies, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and global privacy standards.

2. Data Collection and Usage

SatTrack GPS is engineered with a privacy-first, on-device architecture. Below are details regarding the device features and permissions utilized by the Application:

A. Precise Location Data (GNSS / GPS)

  • Permission Required: ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION
  • Purpose: Used exclusively to access raw GNSS satellite status (GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, BeiDou, QZSS), measure carrier-to-noise density ratio (C/N0), calculate positioning accuracy, and render satellite positions on the 2D Radar, 3D Globe, and AR Sky View.
  • Data Handling: All location coordinates and satellite telemetry are processed 100% on-device in real time. We do not track location history, log coordinates to disk, or transmit location data to external servers or third parties.

B. Camera Access (Augmented Reality Sky View)

  • Permission Required: CAMERA
  • Purpose: Used solely to display a live camera preview background for the AR Sky View mode, allowing users to visualize satellite passes aligned with physical sky coordinates.
  • Data Handling: Optical sensor data is streamed directly to local memory for frame rendering only. The Application does not record video, capture static photos, perform facial or object recognition, or store or transmit camera feeds.

C. Motion and Orientation Sensors (Compass & Gyroscope)

  • Sensors Used: Rotation Vector, Accelerometer, Magnetometer, Gyroscope
  • Purpose: Used to determine device orientation (azimuth heading and pitch angle) so that the AR overlay and 3D compass reticle match your physical direction.
  • Data Handling: Sensor data is evaluated locally on a frame-by-frame basis and is immediately overwritten or discarded. No sensor logs are retained.

D. Advertising & Analytics (Google AdMob)

  • Service Provider: Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK
  • Purpose: The Application displays non-intrusive banner advertisements to support ongoing development.
  • Data Collected by Third Parties: Google AdMob may automatically collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic information, unique device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), and crash data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy to deliver policy-compliant, personalized or non-personalized ads.

3. Data Retention and Security

Because SatTrack GPS does not transmit personal data, user location coordinates, or camera feeds to external servers, we do not maintain central databases of user personal information. All diagnostic calculations take place ephemerally in system memory and cease immediately upon exiting the Application or revoking permissions.

6. Your Choices and Permission Controls

You maintain complete control over the permissions granted to SatTrack GPS. You may grant, modify, or revoke permissions at any time through your Android device settings:

  • Location Permission: Go to Settings > Apps > SatTrack GPS > Permissions > Location to enable or disable location access.
  • Camera Permission: Go to Settings > Apps > SatTrack GPS > Permissions > Camera to enable or disable camera access for AR mode.

Please note that revoking Location or Camera permissions will restrict satellite tracking and AR visualization features, though basic offline or simulated modes will remain functional.
